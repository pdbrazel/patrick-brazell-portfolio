About Me

From the warehouse floor to intelligent systems

I spent nearly a decade leading operations — managing teams, controlling costs, and fixing broken processes. Technology isn’t just something I’m studying. I’m actively experimenting with AI and analytics to solve real business and personal problems.

My career started on the warehouse floor and in logistics operations. I learned how to lead people under pressure, protect margins, and improve systems that move metal, money, and timelines.

Along the way I delivered more than $400,000 in combined annual cost reductions — through smarter hauling strategies, tighter expense control, process redesign, and cross-training that made the operation leaner and more capable.

That foundation is why I’m pursuing a Master of Applied Business Analytics at the University of Arkansas. I want to apply the same intensity I brought to the floor to the problems that data, optimization, and AI can solve at scale.

I’m especially interested in how AI can improve real decisions — load planning, resource allocation, forecasting, and operational design. The projects below are active experiments in that direction.
